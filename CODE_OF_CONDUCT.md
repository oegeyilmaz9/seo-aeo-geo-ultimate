# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SEO-AEO-GEO Ultimate is for practical, evidence-led collaboration. We welcome good-faith questions, bug reports, improvements, and disagreement about methods when it stays focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Distinguish evidence, experience, and speculation.
- Give maintainers and contributors room to learn and improve.
- Protect private data, credentials, and customer information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information, secrets, or customer data.
- Deliberately misleading claims, spam, or abusive issue activity.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not use public issues for security disclosures; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For a conduct concern that should not be public, use GitHub's reporting tools. Maintainers may remove, edit, or close contributions that do not meet this standard.
