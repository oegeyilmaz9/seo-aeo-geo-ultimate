# Current source and control review

Reviewed 2026-10-03. Primary documentation, not live property evidence.

The August 60-source registry and earlier platform snapshot remain historical inputs/fixtures. Their old timestamps are preserved. The active suite manifest points to this current primary-source registry. Unrefreshed market/vendor papers are never treated as current recommendation evidence.

Notable corrections: Google generative-AI reports now have a documented worldwide rollout; Anthropic publishes IP data; Perplexity user-directed fetching generally ignores robots.txt. Keep actual property dimensions, owner choices and outcomes separate.

## Verified primary destinations

- [Optimizing your website for generative AI features on Google Search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): Treat foundational SEO, original people-first content, crawlability, and page experience as Google-specific prerequisites. Google explicitly says llms.txt neither helps nor harms Google Search visibility because Search ignores it; keep it optional for other consumers. Reject fixed chunking, special AI schema, synthetic mentions, and generic AI-only rewrites as mandatory Google requirements.
- [The /llms.txt file](https://llmstxt.org/): Implement the owner-required publisher map and clean Markdown destinations with maintained direct links. Client-specific adoption and engine outcomes must be measured separately.
- [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): Keep AI Overviews and AI Mode as separate surfaces. Require an indexed, snippet-eligible page for Google AI-feature eligibility, while never promise serving. Model Googlebot search access and Google-Extended separately.
- [Generative AI performance report (Search)](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139): Report impressions and supported page/country/date/device dimensions as provider observations. Do not invent query rows; preserve aggregation, reporting timezone, export limitations and unavailable-to-zero export ambiguity.
- [Introducing AI Performance in Bing Webmaster Tools Public Preview](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/): Add a separately labeled, optional Bing first-party citation input. Preserve preview and property-access limitations instead of treating it as universally available.
- [Publishers and Developers - FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq): Separate OAI-SearchBot access from GPTBot training preferences. Treat ChatGPT referral tracking as a reported UTM convention, not a complete attribution system. Use accessibility as an agent-compatibility consideration, not a ranking claim.
- [Does Anthropic crawl data from the web, and how can site owners block the crawler?](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler): Keep training, search and user-directed fetch policies separate; use the published Anthropic IP JSON source for identity verification. Access does not prove citation.
- [Perplexity Crawlers](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers): Use separate bot/user-fetch policies and published identity endpoints. Perplexity-User generally ignores robots.txt on user-requested fetches; robots is not an access security boundary.
- [RFC 9309: Robots Exclusion Protocol](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9309.html): Describe robots.txt as crawler access guidance rather than access authorization. Validate standard syntax separately from vendor-specific support and behavior.
- [Google Search documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates): Maintain current, preview, deprecated, and removed feature states; retain the May 2026 FAQ rich-result removal and revalidate changed merchant properties.
- [Product structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product): Audit visible product pages, Product or merchant listing data, and Merchant Center feeds together while treating eligibility and acceptance as separate evidence.
- [Video sitemaps and alternatives](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/video-sitemaps): Validate video sitemap discovery and update state while keeping video indexing and result eligibility separate from submission.
- [Create a Google News sitemap](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/news-sitemap): Add a publisher lane for article transparency, dates, corrections, media, news sitemap freshness, and policy evidence without inclusion promises.
- [Universal Commerce Protocol specification overview](https://ucp.dev/2026-04-08/specification/overview/): Treat UCP as an emerging optional capability with discovery, operations, authentication, owner, and fallback—not as an SEO or AI-visibility requirement.
- [Powering product discovery in ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/): Add OpenAI product and merchant data to commerce parity/capability review without assuming a recommendation, checkout, or sales outcome.
- [IndexNow protocol documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation): Add a bounded change-notification workflow for added, updated, and deleted URLs with key/endpoint verification, receipts, logs, and no indexing guarantee.
- [Generative AI performance reports in Search Console](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports): The announcement now records worldwide rollout on August 31, 2026. Retain only property-exposed dimensions and distinguish impression reporting from observed answers and citations.
- [Canonical URL methods](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls): Evaluate HTTP canonical headers for non-HTML duplicate content; do not replace canonical decisions with Markdown metadata.
- [Robots.txt introduction](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro): Preserve robots.txt separately from Markdown discovery and authentication.
- [Sitemap overview](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview): Maintain native sitemap protocol and discovery/outcome boundaries.
- [Keyword Planner forecasts and historical metrics](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575): Retain geography/network/window, close-variant aggregation and rounded volume; advertiser competition and forecasts are not organic difficulty or ranking evidence.
- [Google Trends data FAQ](https://support.google.com/trends/answer/4365533?hl=en): Relative normalized sampled interest is not monthly search volume; low-volume zeros and statistical noise require limitations.
- [Search performance overview](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en): Use authorized property query/page metrics with their filters/windows; exposure is not total market demand.
- [Common crawlers and Google-Extended](https://developers.google.com/crawling/docs/crawlers-fetchers/google-common-crawlers): Google-Extended is a product token covering specified training and grounding, with no separate request user agent and no Google Search ranking effect.
- [Anthropic crawler IP source](https://claude.com/crawling/bots.json): Use current published IP data for verification instead of an outdated no-published-ranges assumption.
