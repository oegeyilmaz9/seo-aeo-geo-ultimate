# Page review protocol

Retain canonical/requested URL, capture time, locale/device, raw response, rendered state, page-role assumption, and source of any performance/indexing evidence. For every finding, identify its specialist lane and do not collapse lane-specific measures into an overall score.

Use a page review to triage scope. It does not replace an immutable AEO/GEO target capture, a technical investigation, or a visibility measurement run when those artifacts are required.
