# Comparison evidence protocol

For each material row retain entity, criterion, value, source URL or approved internal source, observed/accessed date, market/plan/version scope, and confidence. Label claims as company-owned, competitor-published, independent, editorial interpretation, or unknown.

Use an “as of” date where facts can change. Require product/legal review for comparative claims, trademarks, screenshots, pricing, security, regulated categories, and public customer proof.
