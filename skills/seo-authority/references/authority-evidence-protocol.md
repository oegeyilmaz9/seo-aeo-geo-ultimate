# Authority evidence protocol

Retain raw and canonical source/target URLs, source domain, link or mention text, link attributes, surrounding context reference, discovered/last-seen dates when available, collection source/method/date, locale, entity, target page, vendor metrics with definitions, and limitations.

Do not infer editorial endorsement from crawl discovery alone. A backlink does not prove source quality, a brand mention does not prove a citation, and a vendor authority metric is not a search-engine score.

Treat link schemes, undisclosed paid links, scaled exchanges, hacked links, deceptive redirects, and fabricated mentions as policy risks. Any outreach or correction plan must be truthful, attributable, and reviewable.
