# Editorial brief protocol

## Minimum brief fields

Record the page/asset, target locale, reader task, page purpose, proposed decision or answer, source-backed claim ledger, required original evidence, structure, internal destinations, media/accessibility needs, CTA, owner, reviewers, and acceptance criteria.

## Claim ledger

For each claim, label it `verified fact`, `approved positioning`, `customer proof`, `editorial interpretation`, `placeholder`, or `unknown`. A verified fact needs its source, scope/date, and reviewer when it is material. Do not turn an unknown into a polished sentence.

## Review checklist

- The visible page tells the reader who is making the claim and under which conditions it holds.
- Each material fact is accurate for the declared locale and date.
- Examples, comparisons, quotations, and testimonials are authentic and authorized.
- Links resolve to useful pages and have meaningful purpose.
- Headings, tables, images, captions, and controls remain understandable in a rendered mobile view and to assistive technology.
- The draft does not make ranking, citation, availability, legal, medical, financial, or performance promises that the source does not support.

## Measurement note

Use a content review or rendered-page check to verify the edit itself. Use Search Console, analytics, or a frozen visibility run only for the distinct behavior each observes. Do not attribute a change in one measure to the edit without a valid comparison design.
