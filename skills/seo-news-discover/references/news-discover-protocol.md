# News and Discover evidence protocol

Capture publication identity, article URL, headline, visible author and credentials, publication/modification time, correction state, primary sources, canonical/directives, large-image delivery, structured data, news sitemap/feed row, paywall/access behavior, and observation time.

Keep publication date, meaningful update, crawl discovery, indexing, news eligibility, Discover appearance, impression, click, subscription, and revenue as separate events. Do not turn a case study or a single report row into a universal freshness rule.

Policy, paywall, canonical, redirect, sitemap/feed publication, and large-scale template changes require an approved action and post-change verification.
