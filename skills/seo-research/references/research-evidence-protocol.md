# SEO research evidence protocol

Every source row contains: claim/question, source title/URL or approved first-party source, source type, locale/context, published/updated date if available, accessed date, collection method, and limitation. Keep source claims distinct from the researcher’s recommendation.

For volatile market/product/platform facts, name the refresh trigger. For third-party keyword or traffic estimates, retain tool/method/date and label them estimates.

For formal query work, use `query-corpus.json`. Preserve one stable need family across its observed search queries, AI prompts, conversation turns, and directly observed executed subqueries. Do not invent engine-executed queries when a surface does not expose them. Keep demand unavailable rather than filling it from intuition, and bind local source and coverage/cannibalization evidence by bundle-relative path plus SHA-256.

When choosing priority query families, follow [query-opportunity-protocol.md](query-opportunity-protocol.md). Preserve owner-named strategic queries, query/page/country/device/search-type/window context from first-party exports, and dated locale/device result-set observations. Show intent fit, business fit, page ownership, authority/evidence gap, demand, effort, and risk separately rather than hiding them in one keyword score.
