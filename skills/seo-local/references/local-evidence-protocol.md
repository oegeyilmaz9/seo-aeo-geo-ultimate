# Local evidence protocol

## Entity and location record

Give every real location, practitioner, department, or service-area business a stable ID. Retain the official name, status, address or service area, phone, website, hours, categories/services, coordinates when authorized, source of truth, platform profile IDs/URLs, last verified time, and owner.

## Query and page mapping

Preserve user location, locale, device, task, query source, and observation date. Map queries to existing pages before proposing a new location page. A missing keyword is not proof that another page should exist.

## Reviews and policy

Use reviews only from authorized/publicly accessible evidence and respect platform terms, privacy, and copyright. Do not recommend review gating, incentives prohibited by the platform, fabricated markup, or policy-evasive profiles.

## Handoff

Profile edits, closures, merges, category changes, hours, redirects, canonicals, and location-page publication require an approved action with a named owner, verification capture, and rollback where possible.
