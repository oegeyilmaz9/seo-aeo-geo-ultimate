# Scaled-page protocol

For each page family record the reader task, entity/data grain, required source fields, source owner/freshness, template behavior, URL/canonical rule, locale rule, publication condition, removal condition, and review sample.

Test normal, absent, contradictory, stale, locale-specific, and edge data. A page remains withheld when its reader task or factual basis is not met. Publish/expand decisions require explicit owner approval and a reversible rollout.
